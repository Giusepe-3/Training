### ⚡ PERFORMANCE EXTRACTION AUDIT — "are we leaving fitness on the table?"

> **Run this FIRST, every Sunday. The point (user directive 2026-07-20):** the coach's fitness model has repeatedly UNDER-estimated the engine — 7/19 benchmark modeled ~7:00/km easy, he ran **6:31**; RHR held build-best (42) through a 17-day layoff. Madrid taught the same lesson from the other side: the true engine was bigger than a conservative read assumed, and performance went un-cashed. This audit is the **upside mirror of the Red Flags block** — Red Flags catch over-reaching; this catches **under-dosing**. Never let the plan lag a fast-adapting engine.
>
> ⚠️ **Guardrail — extraction ≠ recklessness.** For THIS athlete the extraction levers are (1) **cashing latent aerobic fitness** — faster paces at the same HR, checkpoints read for upside — and (2) **not holding loads/paces back on stale caution**. They are NOT hero sessions on the tibia. **The tibia gate and "consistency > hero session" win every tie.** Madrid under-shot from under-*endurance*, not from training sandbag → the fix is keeping the plan abreast of the engine, not adding impact risk.

| # | Question | Trigger = engine is AHEAD of the plan | Ratchet action if triggered |
|---|----------|----------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| 1 | **Easy runs landing easier than modeled?** | Pace at the HR cap ran faster than expected, OR RPE below target, most runs this week | Hold the HR caps (bone-gated), but expect + allow the faster pace. Note the new pace-at-HR baseline. |
| 2 | **Lifts under RPE at held loads?** | All sets RPE ≤6 at a load held for layoff/caution, 2 sessions running | The layoff caution has expired → **bump now**, don't wait the full top-of-range gate. |
| 3 | **Latest checkpoint read for UPSIDE, not just pass/fail?** | W4/W8/W10 test beaten by a clear margin (e.g. W8 6k ≤5:35, or W4 8k >20 s/km faster than 7/18) | A checkpoint smashed = the model was wrong-low → **recalc the target**, don't just tick the box. |
| 4 | **Was the true ceiling probed anywhere this week?** | Every session sat comfortably inside caps; no honest hard effort found a real limit (tibia permitting) | If 2+ weeks with zero ceiling contact and tibia quiet → schedule one honest effort at the next checkpoint. |
| 5 | **Does the GOAL still fit the engine?** | ≥2 of Q1–Q4 fired this week AND last week | Target is soft → **put the stretch goal (sub-2:45) explicitly on the table and tell the user.** Don't silently over-ride sub-2:50. |
