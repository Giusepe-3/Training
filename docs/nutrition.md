# Nutrition — Recomp Block

> Maintenance calories. High protein. Quality food. Slow but real lean gains.

---

## What "Recomp" Means

Recomposition = building muscle and losing (or maintaining) fat at the same body weight. It works best for:
- Trainees with a few years of experience but room to grow (you)
- People at a reasonable body fat starting point (post-race lean = good)
- People not in a rush

You won't see dramatic scale changes. You will see:
- Mirror improvements (chest fuller, shoulders rounder, arms thicker)
- Strength gains (the Excel log will show this clearly)
- Stable body weight, slightly tighter waist, same or larger limb measurements

**Realistic 8-week outcome:** +1.5 to +3 kg lean mass, ±0.5 kg body weight, visibly bigger upper body.

---

## Calorie Target

**Maintenance** — eat the amount of food that holds your weight stable.

If you don't already know your maintenance: track weight every morning (fasted, after bathroom) for 1 week and adjust:
- Weight stable ±0.3 kg over 7 days → you're at maintenance
- Losing > 0.3 kg/week → eat more (add 200 kcal)
- Gaining > 0.5 kg/week → eat slightly less (subtract 150 kcal)

**Rough estimate for your profile (active male, ~70 kg estimated):** 2,600–2,900 kcal/day.

---

## Macronutrient Targets

| Macro | Target | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Protein** | 1.8–2.2 g/kg body weight | Non-negotiable for muscle synthesis. Roughly 130–160 g/day. |
| **Carbohydrate** | 4–6 g/kg | Fuels lifting and the 2 runs. Roughly 280–420 g/day. |
| **Fat** | 0.8–1.2 g/kg | Hormonal health. Don't go below ~60 g/day. |

Hit protein every day. Carbs and fat can flex with appetite and training schedule.

---

## Protein Distribution

Spread protein across 4–5 meals at ~30–40 g per meal. This maximizes muscle protein synthesis. Examples per meal:
- 150 g chicken breast (~32 g protein)
- 200 g Greek yogurt + 30 g whey (~30 g)
- 4 whole eggs + 50 g oats (~28 g)
- 200 g tuna or salmon (~40 g)
- Protein shake post-workout (~30 g)

---

## Training Day vs Rest Day

**Lifting days (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri):**
- Slightly higher carb intake
- Pre-lift: 30–60 g carbs + small protein 1–2 hr before
- Post-lift: 30–40 g protein + 50–80 g carbs within 1–2 hr

**Run days (Wed, Sat):**
- Normal carbs (don't need extra — runs are short)
- Don't eat heavy 2 hr before
- Hydrate well

**Rest day (Sun):**
- Normal eating, slightly lower carbs is fine if appetite is low
- Don't under-eat — recovery happens here

---

## Pre/Post-Workout Specifics

**Pre-lift (1–2 hr before):**
- 30–50 g oats + banana + scoop whey, or
- 2 slices toast + 2 eggs + fruit, or
- Rice + chicken + small portion veg

**Post-lift (within 90 min):**
- 30–40 g protein (whey shake, chicken, fish)
- 50–80 g carbs (rice, potato, oats, fruit)
- A "real meal" beats supplements

**Pre-run:**
- Light. Banana + coffee, or piece of toast. Don't eat heavy.

**Post-run:**
- Normal meal within an hour. Nothing special.

---

## Sleep & Recovery

Nutrition is half the battle. Recovery is the other half:
- **7–9 hours of sleep.** Non-negotiable for hypertrophy.
- Same wake time daily (yes, even weekends).
- Cut alcohol — even 2 drinks suppresses muscle protein synthesis for ~24 hr.
- Caffeine: fine pre-workout, but cut off by 2 PM if you have any sleep issues.

---

## Hydration

- 2.5–3.5 L water/day baseline
- Add 500 ml–1 L on lift days
- Electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) on run days, especially if outdoors in heat

---

## Supplement Stack (Optional, In Order of Priority)

| Supplement | Dose | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| **Creatine monohydrate** | 5 g/day, anytime | Best-evidenced supplement for hypertrophy and strength. No loading needed. |
| **Whey protein** | 30 g, 1–2x/day | Convenience for hitting protein target. Real food preferred. |
| **Vitamin D3** | 1000–2000 IU/day | Most people are deficient, especially indoor lifters. |
| Magnesium glycinate | 200–400 mg before bed | Sleep + muscle recovery. Optional. |
| Omega-3 (fish oil) | 1–2 g EPA+DHA/day | Anti-inflammatory. Optional. |

**Skip:** pre-workouts (caffeine alone is enough), BCAAs (waste of money if protein is hit), test boosters (don't work), fat burners (counterproductive in a recomp).

## What You Can Track in the Log

In `logs/training_log.xlsx` → `Body Metrics` tab:
- Body weight (every morning, fasted)
- Waist circumference (Mondays)
- Chest, arm, thigh circumference (every 2 weeks)

Don't obsess over the scale. Look at the 7-day rolling average and circumferences together.

---

## Red Flags

- **Body weight rising > 0.5 kg/week for 2 weeks:** you're in surplus, not recomp. Cut 150–200 kcal.
- **Body weight dropping > 0.5 kg/week:** you're in deficit. Eat more. Hypertrophy is harder in deficit.
- **Lifts stalling and feeling weak:** under-eating or under-sleeping. Audit both.
- **Persistent fatigue:** carbs too low. Bring them up.
