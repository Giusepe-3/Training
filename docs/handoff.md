## Lifting in the Next Block (Maintenance Mode)

You'll keep some lifting to preserve the muscle you just built. Recommended:
- 2 days/week, ~45 min each
- Day 1: Full body upper-emphasis (bench, row, OHP, lateral, curl, tri)
- Day 2: Full body lower-emphasis (squat or DL pattern, single-leg, hip thrust, calf, core)
- RPE 7. No grinding. Maintain reps and load, don't push.

Why drop to 2 days?
- Running volume goes up significantly
- Recovery budget shifts toward aerobic adaptations
- 2 sessions/week maintains 80–90% of muscle gains for ~3–4 months
- After the next race, you can return to a hypertrophy block again if desired
