## Why this shape

- **The deficit is still endurance, not speed.** 5K PR 25:58 predicts a ~1:59 HM; the actual HM was 2:09. Weekly aerobic volume is the biggest lever, so it rises every cycle and quality stays at one session a week.
- **The bike is aerobic volume that doesn't spend the tibia budget.** Bone caps running km; the engine doesn't need the impact. Cycling held the engine through the dark month (Aug) and the commute (96 km/wk, Sep) — and bike km have been 0 since early Oct.
- **A fixed 5K every 4 weeks** gives a date that guards against dark months (the thing that killed the 50K, sub-2:50 and the 30K) and a fitness reading every cycle.
- **Not every 5K is a PR attempt.** An all-out 5K plus the easy days around it costs about a week. Two of every three are CHECKS (train through). Every third is an ATTEMPT (taper, chosen conditions).

## The bike (Fri PM)

- **Easy only: HR ≤140** (cycling HR sits ~10 bpm under running at the same effort), flat route, 85–95 rpm, easy gears. **No intervals, no hard climbs, no segment chasing** — Tuesday is the one hard session.
- **Duration ramp:** recovery wk 45–60 min · cycle 1 60 → 75 · cycle 2 75 → 90 · cycles 3–4 90 min. Test week: Sun 45–60 min spin after the 5K instead.
- **Never converts into running.** Bike time never raises running km, and a missed ride is gone — no make-up, no extra run.
- **Saturday stays bike-free and run-free.** It protects the long run and the 5K.
- **The ride gives way, never the long run:** quads or hip flexors ≥2 on Sun AM → next ride −15 min. Twice → 45 min and recheck.
- Indoor trainer is fine in bad weather, same HR rule.
- **Log it as load:** PM block `Bike:` duration, km, avg HR. Zero bone cost; cost is quads/hip flexors/systemic recovery.
- **Kcal: bike day ~2650–2700** · other days ~2400 · protein 155–165.

## Paces — reset after every 5K

**P5** = pace of the latest 5K (Oct 31 race sets the first one). Every 5K resets all three.

| Zone | Pace | Use |

| T (threshold) | P5 + 20 s/km | cruise intervals, tempo |
| I (VO2) | P5 − 3 s/km | 800 m – 1.2 k reps |

## The 5K

- **Venue:** Ballerup track (12.5 laps) if dry and wind ≤6 m/s · otherwise Copenhagen parkrun (Saturday, timed) or a flat no-lights road loop · treadmill at 1% only for a CHECK in bad winter weather.
- **Pacing:** km 1 at goal pace, never more than 3 s/km faster. Push from 3 k.
- **Goal:** CHECK = run it honest, take a PR if it comes. ATTEMPT = goal set from the last CHECK plus the trend.
- **CHECK week:** train through. Thu easy, Fri rest or upper gym, race Sat.
- **ATTEMPT week:** trim the week-3 long run by 2 k, Mon gym light upper only, no Fri gym, carbs up Fri, pre-midnight bed Fri. Date moveable ±1 day for wind or rain.
- After every 5K: log it, add it to the dashboard's key-efforts file, reset P5.

## Gates (unchanged from Blocks 2–3)

- **Tibia gates everything.** Any pinpoint shin pain → stop, easy only, flag. That month's 5K is cancelled if it is still there on test day. The bike is allowed through a shin flag (non-impact) unless it hurts on the bike.
- **RPE is a breath signal; distance is set by bone.** Never raise a long run because an easy run felt easy.
- Quads or calves ≥2 on a quality morning → swap to easy, quality moves +1 day or drops.
- **Illness neck check** as before (applies to the bike too). Illness is not drift: repeat, don't compress.
- **A dark week is repeated, never clawed back.** Missed km are gone.
- Sleep <6 h AND RHR +10 → that day easy, quality moves.
