## Why this shape

- **The deficit is still endurance, not speed.** 5K PR 25:58 predicts a ~1:59 HM; the actual HM was 2:09. Weekly aerobic volume is the biggest lever, so it rises every cycle. One quality session a week is on the run (threshold), and one is on the bike (VO2).
- **The bike is aerobic volume that doesn't spend the tibia budget.** Bone caps running km; the engine doesn't need the impact. Cycling held the engine through the dark month (Aug) and the commute (96 km/wk, Sep) — and bike km have been 0 since early Oct.
- **A fixed 5K every 4 weeks** gives a date that guards against dark months (the thing that killed the 50K, sub-2:50 and the 30K) and a fitness reading every cycle.
- **Not every 5K is a PR attempt.** An all-out 5K plus the easy days around it costs about a week. Two of every three are CHECKS (train through). Every third is an ATTEMPT (taper, chosen conditions).

## The bike — 🔁 2026-11-15: Wed easy + Fri VO2

**Why the VO2 goes on the bike:** VO2 is mostly a central adaptation (heart, stroke volume), and that transfers from bike to run. Run VO2 reps (800s at I) are the highest-peak-force sessions in the plan, and the bike gets most of the same stimulus with zero impact. What the bike does NOT train is running economy at 5K pace. That is covered by the Tue threshold, the Thu strides and the test-week sharpener.

**Where:** gym stationary bike or indoor trainer (steady reps, no lights or traffic; log watts if the bike shows them). Outdoors only on a flat loop with no lights.

### Fri — BIKE VO2

- **WU 15 min** easy, including 3×30 s high-cadence spin-ups · **main set** · **CD 10 min** easy. ~50–60 min total.
- **Reps:** RPE 8–9, "hard but repeatable", **the last rep as strong as the first**. Cadence 90–100 rpm (spin, don't grind; grinding loads the quads before Sunday). HR should be climbing to ~170+ by the end of reps 2 onward (bike HR runs ~10 bpm under running, and running max is 200). HR lags in 4-min reps, so RPE and watts govern, not HR.
- **Recoveries:** easy spin, never stopped.

- **Progression gate (slow-steady):** move to the next row only if the last rep held RPE ≤9 and did not fade (cadence and watts held). A faded last rep → repeat the row.
- **The ride gives way, never the long run:** quads or hip flexors ≥2 on Sun AM → next Fri VO2 drops one rep. Twice running → back one row.
- **Shin flag:** the bike is allowed (non-impact) unless it hurts on the bike.

### Wed — EASY SPIN

- **HR ≤140**, flat, 85–95 rpm, easy gears. No intervals, no climbs, no segment chasing.
- **Duration:** cycle 1 40–45 min · cycle 2 45–60 · cycles 3–4 60 min. Test week 30–40.
- Quads or calves ≥2 on Wed AM → 30 min Z1 or skip. It is a recovery ride.

### Both rides

- **Never converts into running.** Bike time never raises running km. A missed ride is gone: no make-up, no extra run.
- **Saturday stays bike-free and run-free.** It protects the long run and the 5K.
- **Log it as load:** PM block `Bike:` duration, km, avg HR (+ watts per rep on Fri). Zero bone cost. The cost is quads, hip flexors and systemic recovery.
- **Kcal: Fri VO2 day ~2650–2700** · Wed spin day ~2500 · other days ~2400 · protein 155–165.

## The 5K

- **Venue:** Ballerup track (12.5 laps) if dry and wind ≤6 m/s · otherwise Copenhagen parkrun (Saturday, timed) or a flat no-lights road loop · treadmill at 1% only for a CHECK in bad winter weather.
- **Pacing:** km 1 at goal pace, never more than 3 s/km faster. Push from 3 k.
- **Goal:** CHECK = run it honest, take a PR if it comes. ATTEMPT = goal set from the last CHECK plus the trend.
- **CHECK week:** train through. Thu easy, Fri rest or upper gym, race Sat.
- **ATTEMPT week:** trim the week-3 long run by 2 k, Mon gym light upper only, no Fri gym, carbs up Fri, pre-midnight bed Fri. Date moveable ±1 day for wind or rain.
- After every 5K: log it, add it to the dashboard's key-efforts file, reset P5.

## Gates (unchanged from Blocks 2–3)

- **Tibia gates everything.** Any pinpoint shin pain → stop, easy only, flag. That month's 5K is cancelled if it is still there on test day. The bike is allowed through a shin flag (non-impact) unless it hurts on the bike.
- **RPE is a breath signal; distance is set by bone.** Never raise a long run because an easy run felt easy.
- Quads or calves ≥2 on a quality morning → swap to easy, quality moves +1 day or drops.
- **Illness neck check** as before (applies to the bike too). Illness is not drift: repeat, don't compress.
- **A dark week is repeated, never clawed back.** Missed km are gone.
- Sleep <6 h AND RHR +10 → that day easy, quality moves.
