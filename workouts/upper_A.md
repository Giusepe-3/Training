# Upper A — Monday

**Focus:** Horizontal push/pull. Chest + back priority.
**Duration:** ~75 min
**Warm-up:** 5 min row or bike + dynamic shoulder/scap drills (band pull-aparts, scap pull-ups, light DB press)

---

## Main Block

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | RPE | Notes |
|---|---------|-------------|-----|-------|
| A1 | Barbell Bench Press | 4 × 5–7 | 7 → 8 | Pause 1 sec on chest. Add weight when you hit 7s across. |
| A2 | Barbell Row (Pendlay or Yates) | 4 × 5–7 | 7 → 8 | Hard pull to lower chest/upper abs. Reset between reps if Pendlay. |
| B1 | Incline DB Press (~30°) | 3 × 8–10 | 8 | Slight pause at chest. Don't bounce. |
| B2 | Lat Pulldown (wide, neutral grip) | 3 × 8–10 | 8 | Pull elbows down and back. Squeeze lats. |
| C1 | Pec Deck | 3 × 12–15 | 8–9 | Slow eccentric, full stretch, controlled squeeze. |
| C2 | Face Pull | 3 × 12–15 | 8–9 | Strict form. Pull to face, elbows high. |
| D1 | Triceps Pushdown (rope or straight bar) | 3 × 10–12 | 8 | Lock elbows at sides. Full stretch at top. |
| D2 | DB Curl | 3 × 10–12 | 8 | Slow eccentric. |

> Pairings (A1/A2, B1/B2, etc.) can be alternated as supersets to save time. Rest 90–120 sec on compounds, 60–90 sec on isolation.

## Form Cues (don't skip these)

- **Bench:** feet planted, shoulder blades retracted and depressed, slight arch, bar over lower chest. Drive feet, not just push.
- **Row:** hinge to ~45°, lats engaged before pull, elbows tucked, full stretch at bottom.
- **Lat Pulldown:** lean back ~10°, elbows pull down toward hips, don't shrug.
- **Lateral movements (fly, lateral raise):** initiate from the muscle, not momentum. If shoulders shrug or back arches, drop weight.
