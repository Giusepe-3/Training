# Upper B — Friday

**Focus:** Vertical push/pull. Shoulders + arms priority.
**Duration:** ~75 min
**Warm-up:** 5 min row + dynamic shoulder warm-up (band dislocates, scap circles, light OHP)

---

## Main Block

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | RPE | Notes |
|---|---------|-------------|-----|-------|
| A1 | Standing Overhead Press (or Seated DB Press) | 4 × 6–8 | 7 → 8 | Strict. Glutes squeezed, no leg drive on standing. |
| A2 | Weighted Pull-up (or Lat Pulldown if cannot weight pull-ups) | 4 × 6–8 | 8 | Full hang to chin over bar. Add load via belt. |
| B1 | DB Shoulder Press (incline ~80°) | 3 × 8–10 | 8 | Slightly inclined version targets front delts hard. |
| B2 | Chest-Supported Cable Row | 3 × 10–12 | 8 | Pull to lower chest/upper abs. Squeeze mid-back. |
| C1 | Cable Lateral Raise (one arm at a time) | 4 × 12–15 | 8–9 | Slow eccentric. **Single biggest lever for shoulder width — don't skip sets.** |
| C2 | Face Pull (high cable, rope) | 3 × 15–20 | 8 | External rotation at top. Rear delts + rotator cuff. |
| D1 | Preacher Curl (EZ bar or DB) | 3 × 8–10 | 8–9 | Full stretch at bottom. No swing. |
| D2 | Skull Crusher (EZ bar) or Overhead Cable Tricep Ext | 3 × 8–10 | 8 | Stretch the long head — keep elbows back/up on overhead variants. |

> Rest 90–120 sec on compounds, 60–90 sec on isolation. Lateral raise = your highest-leverage movement for the shoulder priority. Treat it like a main lift.

## Why Lateral Raise is the Priority

Side delt growth is what makes shoulders look wide. Pressing builds front delts but does very little for the lateral head. **You're doing 4 sets of lateral raises here for a reason — every set matters.** Form > weight:
- Slight forward lean
- Pinky slightly higher than thumb at top
- Stop at shoulder height (not above — trapezius takes over)
- Pause briefly at top contraction
- 3-second eccentric

If you can't hit 12 reps with strict form, drop the weight. Sloppy lateral raises grow traps, not delts.

## Form Cues

- **OHP:** ribs down, glutes squeezed, bar travels straight up, head pushes through at lockout.
- **Pull-up:** hollow body, no kipping. Full hang at bottom. Chest to bar at top.
- **Lateral Raise:** see priority section above.
- **Face Pull:** elbows high, hands separate as they reach face, external rotation at end.
