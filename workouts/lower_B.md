# Lower B — Saturday

**Focus:** Hinge pattern, posterior chain emphasis.
**Duration:** ~70 min
**Warm-up:** 5 min bike + glute bridges, hip openers, light kettlebell deadlifts to groove the pattern

---

## Main Block

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | RPE | Notes |
|---|---------|-------------|-----|-------|
| A1 | Trap Bar Deadlift (or Conventional) | 3 × 4–6 | 7 → 8 | Reset every rep. Don't grind to failure. Add weight when 6s across. |
| B1 | Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 × 8–10 / leg | 8 | Front foot far enough that knee tracks over toe. Slight forward lean for quad bias, upright for glute. |
| B2 | Hip Thrust | 3 × 8–10 | 8 | Pause 1 sec at top. Glute squeeze. Don't over-arch lumbar. |
| C1 | Walking Lunge (DBs) | 3 × 10 / leg | 8 | Long stride. Drive through front heel. Step through, don't step back. |
| C2 | Seated Calf Raise | 4 × 12–15 | 8–9 | Soleus emphasis (bent knee). Pause and stretch each rep. |
| D1 | Ab Roller (knee rollout) | 3 × 8–12 | 8 | Knees down, brace hard, exhale on roll-out. Stop short of lumbar over-extension. **Locked sub** (vs Cable Crunch). |

> Deadlift is the only true heavy compound here. Everything else is moderate-load, high-quality reps. Rest 2–3 min on deadlift, 90 sec elsewhere.

## Why This Day Matters

Even though legs are not your priority, this day is critical for:
1. **Running performance maintenance** — strong hamstrings, glutes, and posterior chain are what kept your form intact for 21.41 km in Madrid. Don't lose them.
2. **Knee/hip durability** for the next half-marathon block — posterior chain weakness is the #1 source of running injuries.
3. **Aesthetic balance** — a big upper body on twig legs looks ridiculous. Maintain.

---

## Sunday Run Considerations

Sunday's run is the day after this session. After Lower B, expect:
- Glutes and hamstrings sore Sun morning
- This is **fine and expected** — easy running flushes the soreness
- If glutes feel tight, walking 5–10 min before running fully warms them up
- Don't push pace Sunday. Easy means easy.

## Form Cues

- **Trap Bar DL:** stand inside the trap bar, hips back and slightly down, drive through heels, finish tall. Don't hyperextend at top.
- **Bulgarian Split Squat:** brace core, control the descent, drive through heel of front foot. Most common error: front foot too close.
- **Hip Thrust:** chin tucked, ribs down, full hip extension, **don't dump lumbar**. The squeeze is glutes, not lower back.
- **Walking Lunge:** long step, knee tracks over toe, torso upright. The challenge is balance + control, not load.

---

## Deadlift Note (Important)

Trap Bar over Conventional unless you specifically prefer conventional. Reasons:
- Lower spinal stress = better recovery for the next session
- Easier to maintain form when fatigued from a full week
- Equally effective for hypertrophy
- Less interference with Sunday's run

If you've been pulling conventional for years and it feels great, stay with it. But if there's any back stiffness from race-week recovery or Lower A, trap bar.
