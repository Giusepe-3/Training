## Form Cues

- **Back Squat:** chest up, brace abs hard before descent, knees track over toes, hips and knees rise together.
- **RDL:** hinge with hips back (not down), bar drags down legs, slight knee bend, stop at point of hamstring tension.
- **Leg Press:** lower back stays pressed against pad, don't let knees collapse inward.
- **Calf Raise:** pause 1–2 seconds at top contraction, full stretch at bottom — half reps don't grow calves.
